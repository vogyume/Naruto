# Naruto
naruto vs sasuke, it's real?


## 🚀 Key Features & Demo Files

This repository contains multiple interactive web applications powered by MediaPipe Hands, allowing you to trigger anime-inspired visual effects using various hand gestures:

* **`naruto.html`
  (Dual Wield Mode):** Enables dual-wielding of legendary techniques.
  Spreads your fingers to trigger Naruto's **Rasengan** with your left hand and Sasuke's **Chidori** with your right hand.

  
* **`new_rasengan.html`
  (Two-Handed Fusion):** Focuses energy using both hands.
  Bringing your hands together shapes and intensifies a massive **Rasengan** at the exact midpoint between your hands.

  
* **`rasengan_shoot.html`
  (Cinematic Projectile Shot):** Features a full-screen projectile attack.
  Clench your hand into a fist to charge a fist-sized **Rasengan**,
  then dynamically spread your fingers to launch it directly at the camera for a cinematic, full-screen blast effect.
